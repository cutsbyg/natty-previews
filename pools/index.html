Asked and answered

Questions

Where do you build pools?

We are in Thompsontown and we build across Central Pennsylvania, so most of our pools are within about an hour of the shop. Carlisle, Newville and Duncannon are all jobs we finished in 2024.

What kind of pool can you put in?

Vinyl, fiberglass, tiny and above ground. Vinyl gives you the most freedom on shape and size. Fiberglass goes in fastest.

Can I see the pool before you dig?

Yes. We include a 3D rendering of your backyard at no extra cost, so you can see the pool and the space around it before construction starts.

How long have you been doing this?

Superior Pools has been family owned and operating since 1993, and between us we have over 40 years of pool building experience.

What else can you do while you are here?

Patios, pavilions, fencing, outdoor lighting, retaining walls, plantings and yard repair. Our team handles every step of the build, from excavation to plumbing.

Do you look after the pool after it is in?

We do openings and closings, weekly cleaning and chemicals, filter repairs and service calls, and we carry parts and chemicals at the shop in Thompsontown.
