Questions

Do you come out at night and on weekends?

Yes. The phone is answered 24 hours a day, and that includes nights, weekends and holidays. It is the same number you would ring at nine in the morning.

What does it cost to find out what is wrong?

Nothing. You get a clear, upfront estimate with no hidden fees before any work starts, and we explain what caused it.

Do you replace well pumps and pressure tanks?

Yes. Well pumps, pressure tanks and water treatment systems are a big part of what we do, along with water heaters and main water and sewer line replacements.

What towns do you cover?

We work out of Chambersburg and cover the towns around it — Shippensburg, Waynesboro and Greencastle on the Pennsylvania side, and over the line into Hagerstown in Maryland. If you are near those and not sure, ring and ask.

My drain keeps blocking even after I clear it. Why?

A drain that keeps blocking usually means something deeper: buildup in the pipe, tree roots, or a line that has partly collapsed. We put a camera down it and tell you which one it is.

Should I pour a chemical drain cleaner down it?

We would rather you did not. Those cleaners eat at pipes over time, especially PVC, and they rarely shift a serious blockage. Snaking or hydro jetting is safer and works better.
