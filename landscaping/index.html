Questions

What do you build?

Paver patios and walkways, retaining walls and sitting walls, outdoor kitchens, fire pits and fireplaces, paver driveways, pool decks, stone veneer, boulder walls, fencing and landscape lighting. We also clean and restore paver patios that have gone tired.

How far do you travel?

We are based in Landisburg, in Perry County, and we work right across Central Pennsylvania. Carlisle, Mechanicsburg, Camp Hill, Harrisburg, Enola, New Cumberland, Dillsburg, Hershey and northern York County are all regular runs for us.

What does a job cost?

The projects we take on run from about $2,000 up to $150,000. A small courtyard patio and a full pool deck with a pavilion are very different jobs, so the quote is free and it is the only honest way to answer this.

Are you licensed?

Yes. Our Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor number is PA097483. We are also ICPI certified for paver installation, NCMA certified for segmental walls, and a Techo-Bloc Pro contractor.

Who actually shows up?

Adam owns the business and is on the job site every day, working alongside the crew. The business started in 2005 and Adam took it over in 2012.

Why is it called Jesse James?

Jesse James Reisinger started the business in 2005. Adam worked for him, and when Jesse and his family moved to North Carolina in 2012 Adam took it on. The name stayed because Jesse had built a good reputation with it.
