Commonly asked questions

Do you do state inspection and emissions?

Yes — inspection and emissions on your vehicle, both done here. Call and we will tell you when we can get you in.

Do you sell and fit tires?

Yes. Name brand tires mounted and balanced in our own bays, and we carry Hankook. The size you need is on the sidewall of the tire you have now, or on the sticker in the driver’s door jamb — read it to us and we will price it.

What else do you work on?

Brakes and rotors, oil and filter changes, scheduled maintenance and pre-trip checks, steering and suspension, and general repair. Complete car care is what the sign says and it is what we do.

Where are you, and what towns do people come from?

300 Fickes Lane in Newport — the old Wentzel feed mill site, which is where the name comes from. Customers come to us from Newport, Millerstown, Marysville and Duncannon, and from across Perry County.

When are you open?

Weekdays, from 7:30 in the morning. Call for Saturday hours.
