Commonly asked questions

Do you do state inspection and emissions?

Yes. Zimmerman’s is a licensed Pennsylvania inspection station and an emissions station, so safety inspection and emissions are both done here. Cars, motorcycles, trailers and medium trucks.

What do you work on?

Every make and model, foreign and domestic. Engine and computer diagnostics, brakes, belts and hoses, exhaust, tune-ups, tires and routine maintenance — and hybrid vehicles too. The shop runs twelve bays with ASE certified technicians.

Do I need an appointment for an oil change?

No. The Rapid Lube is a separate drive-in bay for oil, filter and fluid changes while you wait. Pull in during Rapid Lube hours and we will take care of it.

Can you install a child safety seat?

Yes — child safety seat installation is one of the things this shop is known for. Call ahead and we will set a time.

How long have you been here?

Since 1958, at 2234 South Market Street in Mechanicsburg. Norman Zimmerman started the shop as a gas station and garage; it is still family owned and run by the second generation today.

When are you open?

The service shop is open Monday through Friday, 8:00 in the morning until 5:00, and Saturday 9:00 until 4:00. Closed Sunday.
