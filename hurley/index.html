    Questions

    Do you come out at night or on a holiday?

    Yes. Our hours are 24 hours a day, every day of the year, holidays included. Call (717) 678-7550 and you get us.

    Do you install whole-house generators?

    Yes. Whole-house generators are one of the jobs written on the side of our vans, along with solar. We size the unit, install it and wire it in.

    Can you upgrade my electrical panel?

    Yes. Panel upgrades and new services are among our most common jobs. The photographs above show one from the trench in the garden right through to the meter on the wall.

    What areas do you cover?

    We work across Central Pennsylvania from two shops, one in Mechanicsburg and one in Harrisburg, and we cover Cumberland, Dauphin, Adams, Lancaster, Lebanon, Perry and York counties. If you are near the edge of that, ring and ask.

    Are you licensed?

    Yes. Our Pennsylvania licence number is 031867, and our electricians are licensed and insured. Ask any electrician for their number before they start.

    Will I know the price before you start?

    Yes. We give you clear, open dialogue every step of the way so there are no surprises, and coming out to look and price the job is free.
