Commonly asked questions

Do you do state inspection and emissions?

Yes — Lewis Auto Care is a licensed Pennsylvania inspection station, and emissions testing is done here too. Cars, trailers, and medium and heavy trucks.

Do you work on BMW, Mercedes and other European cars?

Yes. European vehicles are what the shop is known for — BMW, Mercedes-Benz, Volvo and the rest — and every other make and model is welcome as well.

Can you look at my check engine light?

Bring it in. A trouble code names the circuit, not the part, so the work is finding out which one — and that diagnosis is what this shop is known for.

Do you take fleet vehicles?

Yes. Fleets are welcome, and they have been for years — it says so on the sign out front.

Where are you, and what towns do people come from?

700 S Enola Road, Unit A, in Enola. Customers come from Enola and Harrisburg, and from across Cumberland County.

When are you open?

Monday through Friday, 8:00 in the morning until 6:00 at night. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
