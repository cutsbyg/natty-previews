Questions

Questions we get.

Do I need an appointment for auto repair in Glen Rock?

Yes — book online with the form below or call (717) 235‑9607. Booking lets us have the right parts on the shelf when your vehicle arrives.

Do you do PA state inspection and emissions?

We do both. Bring the vehicle in and we handle the safety inspection and the emissions test in the same visit.

What are your hours?

Monday through Friday, 7:00 AM to 5:30 PM. We are closed Saturday and Sunday.

Is the repair guaranteed?

We are a NAPA AutoCare Center, so qualifying parts and labor carry the NAPA 3‑year, 36,000‑mile nationwide warranty — honored at NAPA AutoCare shops across the country, not only here.

What makes of vehicle do you work on?

We are a full-service shop for Asian and American cars, trucks and SUVs, and we handle general inspections and minor service for European makes.

Which towns do you serve?

We are on Theatre Road in Glen Rock, about half a mile north of the Markets at Shrewsbury, so most of our customers drive in from Shrewsbury, New Freedom, Stewartstown and the rest of southern York County.
