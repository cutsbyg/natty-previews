Before you call

Questions we get asked

Do you repair roofs, or only replace them?

Both. We do repairs, full replacements, and storm and wind damage — and we hang gutters, siding and windows too.

What goes on a full replacement?

Architectural shingle over black underlayment — the black in the photo above is the underlayment stage, not a finished roof. We also do ventilation and inspections.

Do you do commercial roofs?

No. Residential services only — houses, not warehouses.

What areas do you cover?

We work out of Newport and cover Perry, Cumberland and Dauphin counties. Marysville, Carlisle, Camp Hill, Mechanicsburg, New Cumberland and Harrisburg are all regular runs, so if you are near any of them, call and ask.
