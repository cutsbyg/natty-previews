Commonly asked questions

What towns do you serve?

Elliottsburg is home, and the truck runs across Perry County from there — Loysville, Landisburg, Blain, Shermans Dale, New Bloomfield, Ickesburg and Newport. If you are near any of them, call and ask.

Do you really do both electrical and plumbing?

Both, out of the same shop, since 2006. One call covers the wire and the water instead of waiting on two trades to line up.

Do you charge for the estimate?

No. Estimates on electrical and plumbing work are free. Call (717) 594-2089 or send the job through the form below.

What are your hours?

6:30 in the morning to 5 in the afternoon, Monday through Friday. Closed Saturday and Sunday.

Do you work on farms and businesses too?

Yes — residential, commercial and agricultural. Barns, shops and outbuildings get the same work as the house.
