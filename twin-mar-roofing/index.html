Before you call

Questions we get asked

What kind of roofs do you put on?

Four. Asphalt shingle — we often use GAF shingles, but will work with any brand the customer prefers. Metal, including standing seam. Rubber, for porch roofs and other low-sloped areas where shingles aren’t the best option. And cedar shakes, for those who want a natural look.

Do you repair roofs, or only replace them?

Both. New roof installation, full replacement and repair, including storm damage.

Do you do commercial roofs?

No. We are a residential roofing company serving central Pennsylvania — houses, not warehouses.

What areas do you cover?

We are a residential roofer based in Millerstown, and we are happy to serve Mechanicsburg, Linglestown, Camp Hill, Harrisburg, State College and surrounding areas — Mechanicsburg to Chambersburg. Recent jobs have been in Halifax, Burnham, Lykens and Camp Hill, so if you are near any of them, call and ask.
