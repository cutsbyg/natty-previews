Commonly asked questions

Do you come to me?

Yes — that is the whole business. The tools come to the car: your driveway, the lot at work, or wherever it is sitting. Call and tell us where you are.

What towns do you cover?

Harrisburg, Camp Hill, Mechanicsburg, Carlisle, York and Allentown, and across Dauphin, Cumberland and Lancaster counties. Call and we will tell you if you are in range.

What can you do at my house?

Brakes and rotors, oil changes, batteries and electrical, steering and suspension, flat tires and wheels, diagnostics, engine repair and general maintenance. Describe the job on the phone and we will tell you whether it is one we can do where the car sits.

Can you look at a used car before I buy it?

Yes. We do pre-purchase inspections — we will meet you at the seller and go over the car with you before any money changes hands.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 9 to 5. Saturday, 10 to 2. Closed Sunday.
