Commonly asked questions

Where are you, and when are you open?

1216 S. York Street in Mechanicsburg. The market is open all year, six days a week — Monday to Friday 9:00 to 6:00 and Saturday 9:00 to 5:00, closed Sunday. Hours move with the season, so call before a long drive. No pets and no smoking anywhere on the farm.

What does it cost to get into the FarmPark?

Spring and summer, $7.50 a person. Fall is $8.50 Monday to Friday and $10.50 on Saturday and Sunday. Late fall is $7. Admission is per person for ages 2 to 70, and last admission is 45 minutes before closing.

What animals are there?

Scottish Highland cattle, pygmy goats, sheep, rabbits, turkeys, chickens, Muscovy ducks and pigs. The flock started as one sheep in 1991.

What’s in the market?

Locally grown fruit and vegetables, home-raised beef, fresh cut meats and sausages, baked goods, homemade soups and deli salads, mixes, candies and frozen take-and-make meals. Soft serve and hand-dipped ice cream, and fresh sandwiches from FarmSide Eats.

Can we pick our own?

Yes, in season — a pick-your-own flower field, a strawberry patch and a pumpkin patch. U-pick pumpkins and the Pumpkin Chunkin’ cannon carry an extra fee in October.

Do you do birthday parties, school trips and camp?

Yes. Birthday parties and group events, guided school field trips on a working farm, and a summer farm camp. Send us the date and we’ll tell you what’s open.
