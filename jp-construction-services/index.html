Commonly asked questions

What roofing work do you do?

Roof installation, replacement, repair, maintenance and inspection — plus storm damage restoration and emergency roofing when the weather does not wait. We are a GAF certified roofing contractor.

Do you only do roofs?

No. Roofing is the core of it, but we are a full construction outfit: additions, basements, framing, siding, doors and windows, concrete, tile, kitchen and bathroom remodeling, plumbing and electrical.

What areas do you cover?

We are based on Montebello Road in Duncannon and work across Central Pennsylvania — Harrisburg, Colonial Park, Steelton, Middletown, Hershey, Mechanicsburg, Camp Hill, Carlisle, Elizabethtown, Lebanon, Lancaster and York.

Do you handle storm damage and insurance claims?

Yes. Storm damage restoration and emergency roofing are part of what we do, and we work with insurance claims. Call and we will get eyes on the roof.

Are you licensed and insured?

Fully licensed and insured — Pennsylvania contractor licence PA159617.
