What the missed calls cost.

Two numbers from the owner. The rest is arithmetic he can check in his head.

$
A week 10 calls $350
A month 40 calls $1,400
A year 480 calls $16,800

week × 4 = month  ·  month × 12 = year

An estimate, not a quote — it is his own two numbers on a four-week month. If he says he only misses a couple, put in a couple; the number is his, which is what makes it land.
