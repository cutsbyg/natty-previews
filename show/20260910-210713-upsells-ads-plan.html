The ad that works: one shape, three variants

Format

10 to 20 seconds, vertical, real barber, real chair, same angle before and after. Storefront stills lose. Review-screenshot cards and team photos beat stock.

One offer, said once

"$5 off your first cut, say you saw this" or "free beard line-up with a cut this week." A discount on a $30 cut is cheap against a client worth about $510 a year at a three-week cadence.

One button

Call now to the receptionist, which books it. Book only where the shop has a page. Call now wins for phone-only shops, which is most of our list.

Targeting and variants

Five miles, men 18 to 55, scheduled to shop hours for call ads, a lead form outside them. Three variants per shop: (A) before/after cut, (B) the barber talking to camera for eight seconds, (C) the review card. Kill the worst after seven days.

Where not to cut

  • The video. A phone clip is fine; a stock image is not, and a shaky vertical in bad light loses to the shop next door.
  • Listing photos and review replies. They are the whole organic engine.
  • Hours-scheduling on call ads. A call into a closed shop with the receptionist off is a wasted lead.
  • The tracking number. Without it "booked per dollar" is a promise, not a number.

All-in for one shop

The shop pays $300 to $450 a month of spend on its own card (Meta $10 a day plus Local Services $50 to $75 a week) and $99 to us. Our marginal cost is $1.15 and an hour. The margin is the same 85%+ every other line carries.
