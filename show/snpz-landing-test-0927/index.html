How it works

Three calls, all to your own phone.

  1. Have it call you

    Type your mobile and it rings you in about a minute, answering as a barbershop.

  2. Connect your Square

    One tap on Square's page. It learns your barbers, your prices and your hours, and calls you back as your shop.

  3. Let it book you a call with us

    On that call it books a 30-minute set-up call straight into your own Square calendar. That's the whole product, on your own phone.

What barbers ask us

My clients won't want to talk to a robot.

Then set it to take only the calls you miss. Your phone rings first every time, and anyone who asks for a person gets put through to you.

I already answer my own phone.

Keep doing that. It never touches a call you pick up. It's there for when you're mid-fade, closed or off.

What if it books something wrong?

It books out of your own Square calendar, with each barber's days and breaks, and texts you every booking it makes.

I use Booksy, not Square.

On Square it books into the calendar you already use. On Booksy it can't get into your calendar, so it answers and takes a message for you.

What if it can't answer something?

It never makes up a price or a time. It passes the call to the number you pick.

How do I cancel?

Text us and billing stops that day. There's no contract.

We're walk-in only.

If your phone rarely rings, you probably don't need us. If people call to ask about the wait, it can tell them to come on in.
