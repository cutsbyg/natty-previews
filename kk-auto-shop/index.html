Commonly asked questions

Do you do collision and paint work?

Yes — that is the main of it. Paint and collision repairs, panel work and complete body restoration. Call and we will tell you when we can get you in.

Do you do state inspection and general repair?

Yes. General repairs, state inspections and diagnostics are all done here, alongside the body work.

Do I have to use the shop my insurance tells me to use?

No. The choice of repair shop is yours, not your insurer’s. Call us about your job either way.
