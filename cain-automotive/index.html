Commonly asked questions

Do you do state inspection and emissions?

Yes — we are a licensed Pennsylvania safety inspection station and an OBD emissions station, so both are done here in one stop. Call and we will tell you when we can get you in.

What do you work on?

All major and minor automotive repair, foreign and domestic. Brakes, engine and computer diagnostics, exhaust, cooling, steering and suspension, transmission and clutch work, tires, and routine maintenance.

Can you look at my check engine light?

Yes. A code names the circuit, not the part — working out which part it is is the job. Bring it in and we will tell you what we find.

Where are you, and what towns are you near?

301 South Enola Drive in Enola, Cumberland County. We are on the west shore, minutes from Marysville, Camp Hill, Lemoyne, Wormleysburg and Harrisburg.

When are you open?

Weekdays, from 8 in the morning. Call before you drive over and we will tell you what time we close that day.
