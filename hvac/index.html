Questions

Do you work on heat pumps and ductless mini-splits?

Yes. We sell and service heat pumps, and we install Mitsubishi Electric ductless mini-split systems. We are also a Bryant dealer for furnaces, boilers and air conditioning.

Where do you work?

We work in Carlisle and the surrounding communities of Cumberland County, Pennsylvania. The shop is on Bolton Avenue in Carlisle.

Do you put in water heaters?

We fit Bradford White tank water heaters. We hard pipe every heater with copper tubing, with no flexible connectors and no quick connect fittings.

Can you do anything about the air in the house?

We sell and install humidifiers and air purifiers, including the RGF Halo LED and REME Halo whole home in-duct purifiers that fit inside your ductwork.

When are you open?

Monday to Friday, 8:00 in the morning until 4:30 in the afternoon. We are closed Saturday and Sunday.
