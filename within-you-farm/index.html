Commonly asked questions

Where are you, and when are you open?

1849 Fisher Road, Mechanicsburg. The farm market is open Friday from 2 to 5 in the afternoon and Saturday from 10 until noon.

What do you sell?

Lavender essential oil, hydrosol and sprays, culinary lavender, soap, salve and lip balm, along with home goods and a range of other locally and nationally sourced products in the market.

Is the lavender actually grown here?

Yes. The field was planted in 2021 and holds about 900 plants across five French and English cultivars. It is hand-harvested in late summer and steam distilled on the farm in small batches.

Can I book a photography session in the field?

Photography sessions and mini sessions are booked through the farm. Call first and ask what the field is doing — it changes a great deal through the season.

Do you host events?

Yes. We take inquiries for indoor and outdoor events in the farm market and in the lavender field. Call and tell us what you have in mind.

Do you ship?

Yes. The full range ships, and shipping is free on orders over $100.
