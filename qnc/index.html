We won best custom home.

Every year the builders' association for Cumberland, Dauphin, Juniata and Perry counties judges the new houses built here. QnC came home with five of the 2026 awards.

  1. 1 — The awards

    Best Custom Home: Under $1,000,000 — 2026 Pyramid Awards, Home Builders Association of Metropolitan Harrisburg. With it came Best Residential Renovation and Best Addition, both under $100,000, Best Specialty Space under $125,000, and Best Outdoor Living under $100,000.

  2. 2 — The house that won

    The house on the first screen of this page. It is the one that took Best Custom Home under a million dollars.

  3. 3 — The one the visitors picked

    People's Choice Award, 2025 Parade of Homes. That one is not judged — it is voted for by the people who walked through the house.
