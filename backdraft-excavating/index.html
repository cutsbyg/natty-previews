Before you call

Commonly asked questions

What kind of work do you take on?

Full service excavating — basements, grading, site prep, septic and sand mound systems and more. We also do construction site prep and trenching, driveways, retaining walls and fences, concrete, hauling, landscaping and mowing, tree removal and stump grinding, and snow removal.

Can you fix a wet yard?

Yes. Drainage and grading is a big part of what we do. We cut the drain line, set the pipe and a catch basin, run it out to daylight, then regrade and straw it back.

Do you do septic and sand mound systems?

Yes — septic and sand mound systems are part of our full service excavating, along with basements, grading and site prep.

Are you registered in Pennsylvania?

Yes. We hold Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor registration PA112106, which is the state registration a contractor needs to do home improvement work here.

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Duncannon and serve communities throughout Perry County, Dauphin County and Cumberland County, and the surrounding region. Our team knows the terrain and soil conditions across Central Pennsylvania.
