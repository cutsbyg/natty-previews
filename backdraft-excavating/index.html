Before you call

Questions we get asked

What kind of excavating do you do?

Basements, grading and site prep, trenching, driveways, retaining walls and fences, and septic and sand mound systems. We run our own backhoes and a skid steer.

My yard holds water. Can you fix it?

That is most of what we do. We cut the drain line, set the pipe and a catch basin, run it out to daylight, then regrade the ground and straw it back over the top.

Do you do anything besides dirt work?

Yes. Concrete — walks, retaining walls, floors and slabs. Hauling of materials and equipment. Landscaping and lawn maintenance — mowing, trimming, hardscaping and mulch. Tree trimming, removal and stump grinding. And snow removal through the winter, residential and commercial.

What areas do you cover?

We work out of Duncannon and cover Perry, Dauphin and Cumberland counties and the surrounding region. Marysville, Newport, New Bloomfield, Shermans Dale, Halifax, Harrisburg, Camp Hill, Mechanicsburg and Carlisle are all regular runs, so if you are near any of them, call and ask.
