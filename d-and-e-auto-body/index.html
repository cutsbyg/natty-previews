Commonly asked questions

What kind of work do you take on?

Collision repair, rust repair and refinishing, and custom paint. Cars, trucks and motorcycles — from crash damage to a full custom flame job.

Can you match my factory paint?

Matching paint is the heart of body work and we do it on every repair — the job is not done until the new panel disappears into the old ones.

Do you do custom paint?

Yes — hand-laid flame jobs, kandy colors and motorcycle tins are some of our favorite work. Bring us the idea and we will talk through what it takes.

How long have you been at this?

D & E Auto Body has been fixing and painting vehicles in Newport since 1975.

What areas do you cover?

We are on Old Limekiln Lane in Newport and work for people across Perry County and the surrounding area.
