Commonly asked questions

Do you do state inspection and emissions?

Yes. We are a licensed Pennsylvania inspection station — the safety inspection and the OBD emissions test on your car, both done here. Call and we will tell you when we can get you in.

Can you look at my check engine light?

Yes. We read the codes and work from there. A code names the circuit, not the part — finding out which part it is is the job.

Do you do oil changes and general repair?

Yes. Oil and filter changes, and general auto repair. Tell us what it is doing and we will tell you what we can do.
