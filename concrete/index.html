Ask us anything

Common questions

What areas do you pour in?

We work out of McAlisterville in Juniata County and cover central Pennsylvania and the communities around it. Over the years our flatwork crews have also worked in Maryland and West Virginia. If you are not sure whether you are close enough, call and ask.

What kinds of concrete do you do?

Driveways, patios, sidewalks and curbs, walkways, garden paths, steps and tiers, stamped stoops and borders, fire pits, retaining walls, pool decks, basements, concrete repairs and resealing. We also place concrete for commercial and industrial jobs.

How does a job start?

We meet you at the site, talk about how you want to use the space, and take measurements so the estimate is real. Then we show you samples of recent work and give you a written proposal with the scope, the costs, the materials and a timeline.

How is stamped concrete made?

We pour the slab, then press stamping tools into the surface to give it a texture and a pattern before it hardens. Colour goes in during mixing or on top of the fresh pour. You pick the pattern and the colour before we start.

How do I look after stamped concrete?

Wash it now and then with mild detergent and water, and have it resealed every two to three years. That is the whole job. We reseal concrete we poured and concrete we did not.

Are you licensed, and do you stand behind the work?

Yes. We are Pennsylvania Contractor PA-002867 and we have been pouring concrete since 1968. All work is backed by our 100% satisfaction guarantee.
