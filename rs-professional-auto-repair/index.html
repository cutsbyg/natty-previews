Commonly asked questions

Do you do state inspection and emissions?

Yes — a licensed Pennsylvania safety inspection and emissions station, both done in our own bays. Call and we will tell you when we can get you in.

Can you look at my check engine light?

Yes. A code names the circuit, not the part — so we read it, then test to find out which piece of that circuit actually failed before anything gets replaced.

What do you work on?

Maintenance, repairs and state inspections. Brakes, oil and filter changes, tires, batteries and starters, air conditioning, radiators and cooling, engine and transmission work, and general repair on cars and light trucks.

Where are you, and what towns do people come from?

131 North Cameron Street in Harrisburg, just off the interstate. Customers come to us from Harrisburg, Mechanicsburg, New Cumberland, Middletown and Hershey.

When are you open?

Weekdays 8 to 5, and Saturday 8 to 2. Closed Sunday.
