Questions we get asked

What tree work do you do?

Tree removal is what we are known for, and we also do tree trimming, tree felling, land clearing and stump grinding.

Can you take a tree down in a tight spot?

That is the job people call us for. Our climbers take a tree down one limb at a time when it is close to a house, a shed or a fence, and we bring a crane in when the drop zone needs one.

What happens to the mess afterwards?

Our ground crew cuts up and hauls off what comes down and leaves your property neat and tidy. We run our own trucks, chippers and saws, and we grind the stumps.

Do you charge for the estimate?

No. Estimates on tree work are free, and we are fully licensed and insured — PA Lic 144520.

What areas do you cover?

We work out of Shermans Dale and cover Perry County — Duncannon, New Bloomfield and Marysville — plus the Cumberland Valley boroughs of Carlisle, Mechanicsburg, Camp Hill and Schlusser. If you are near any of them, call and ask.
