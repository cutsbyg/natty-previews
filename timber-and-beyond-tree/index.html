Before you call

Questions we get asked

What tree work do you do?

We specialize in tree removal, and we also do tree trimming, tree felling, land clearing and stump removal.

Can you take a tree down in a tight spot?

That is the job we are known for. Our crew are experienced climbers who take a tree down one limb at a time when it is in a really tight spot or close to buildings, and we bring a crane in when the drop zone needs one.

What happens to the mess afterwards?

Our ground crew cleans up after the tree is down and leaves your property neat and tidy. We run our own trucks, chippers and saws, and we grind stumps.

What areas do you cover?

We work out of Shermans Dale and cover Perry County plus the Cumberland Valley boroughs — Carlisle, Mechanicsburg and Camp Hill. Duncannon, New Bloomfield, Marysville, Schlusser, Harrisburg and Hershey are regular runs too, so if you are near any of them, call and ask.
