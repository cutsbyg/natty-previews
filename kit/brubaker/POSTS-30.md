## The backbone — how every post is built

Every post that works in this niche has six parts, and we write all six before anyone hits record:

1. **VERBAL HOOK** — the first line out of your mouth. It names the viewer's problem in under 4 seconds.
2. **VISUAL HOOK** — what fills the screen in the first 2 seconds. Motion, metal, or a cow. Never a logo.
3. **TEXT HOOK** — the caption burned on screen, because most feeds autoplay on mute.
4. **OPEN** — how the first 10 seconds earn the next 30. A promise, a question, or visible tension.
5. **CLOSE** — how it ends and what you ask for. Every post ends with one clear next step.
6. **VALUE** — the one thing the viewer walks away knowing. If there isn't one, don't post it.

**Reference note:** every link below is a real, live video. View counts and durations were pulled directly from each platform on **2026-08-24**. Ten of the thirty (marked **[GEMINI]**) were downloaded and run frame-by-frame through our AI video analyser — the hooks, opens and closes in those entries are transcribed from the actual footage, not guessed. The other twenty were broken down from viewing and page data.

**Your kit:** phone, a $30 tripod, a $25 wireless lapel mic, and your own yard. Every one of these is filmable by one person. Nothing here needs a second person to react on cue.
