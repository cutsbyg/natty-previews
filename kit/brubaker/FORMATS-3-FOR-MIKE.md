# Three video formats for Double B — ready to film

Every video we make runs on the same backbone: a **hook** in the first two seconds (what you *see*, what you *hear*, what you *read*), an **open** that earns the next 20 seconds, a **close** that tells people what to do, and one piece of real **value** so the viewer leaves smarter. Here are three built for your yard, with the shot-by-shot playout.

---

## FORMAT 1 — "Why your cows stop here" (~40 seconds)
*Based on a proven Arrowquip demo format — analyzed frame-by-frame.*

- **VISUAL HOOK:** low camera at cow height, pushing slowly up the inside of the alley — the cow's-eye view. Nobody films from down there; it stops the scroll.
- **VERBAL HOOK (first words):** "Nine out of ten cattle that balk at a chute are balking at the same thing — and it isn't the squeeze."
- **TEXT HOOK (on screen):** WHY YOUR COWS STOP HERE
- **OPEN:** tease the answer without naming it — curiosity carries the first 15 seconds.
- **CLOSE:** name the 3E doors, then: "Come stand in it yourself — 717-649-6929."
- **VALUE:** one real design detail and exactly why it changes cattle behavior.

**How it plays out:**
| Time | What's on screen | What Mike says |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:03 | Cow's-eye push down the alley | the verbal hook, over the shot |
| 0:03–0:15 | Same push continues toward the headgate | "It's what she *sees*. Shadows, gaps, daylight through the sides…" |
| 0:15–0:30 | Cut to standing shot at the Arrowlock 88, hand on the 3E doors, open/close them | "…these doors kill the daylight in front of her. Calm cow, one pass." |
| 0:30–0:40 | Wide of the whole chute, Mike beside it | the close + phone number |

**Filming:** phone on a tripod (or held at knee height), two shots, one take each.

## FORMAT 2 — "One hand. One lever." (15 seconds, silent)
*Based on a homemade-headgate clip that did 184,000 views — proof this format prints.*

- **VISUAL HOOK:** the video opens with the cow's head ALREADY moving into the gate. No setup, no intro — action before the viewer decides to scroll.
- **VERBAL HOOK:** none. Silent. (Most feeds autoplay muted — this format doesn't care.)
- **TEXT HOOK:** ONE HAND. ONE LEVER.
- **OPEN:** there is no build-up — the video *is* the moment, and at 15 seconds it loops 2–3 times before the viewer notices.
- **CLOSE:** hard cut at the instant the gate catches. No outro — the hard cut is what makes the loop feel seamless.
- **VALUE:** proof the catch is instant, quiet, and one-man.

**How it plays out:**
| Time | What's on screen |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:02 | Head entering the gate (start the clip mid-motion) |
| 0:02–0:08 | The catch: one hand, one lever, gate closes |
| 0:08–0:14 | Cow standing calm, head out, chewing |
| 0:14–0:15 | Hard cut to black — loop restarts |

**Filming:** tripod tight on the headgate, run one animal, done. Film a different chute off the lot each week — this format repeats forever.

## FORMAT 3 — "20 head? Start here." (~90 seconds)
*Based on a small-farm Bud Box explainer with 99,000 views.*

- **VISUAL HOOK:** Mike, close-up selfie, talking straight down the lens. No equipment in the first shot — a face talking directly at you is the hook.
- **VERBAL HOOK:** "If you've got twenty head and no way to work 'em, you don't need a bigger corral. You need eighteen feet of the right steel."
- **TEXT HOOK:** 20 HEAD? START HERE.
- **OPEN:** the first sentence qualifies the viewer — every small operator watching knows it's for them, so they stay through the setup.
- **CLOSE:** warm, not salesy: "Safer for you, safer for the cattle. Come walk it with me before you buy anything." 
- **VALUE:** a handling layout a 20-head operator can actually afford, laid out on the ground in front of them.

**How it plays out:**
| Time | What's on screen | What Mike says |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:15 | Handheld selfie in the yard | the verbal hook, then "here's the whole setup" |
| 0:15–0:45 | Cut to tripod wide: the Portable 75 chute–alley–tub | walk it end to end, pointing: "cattle come in here… turn here… caught here" |
| 0:45–1:15 | Closer shots at each piece as he touches it | one sentence per piece — what it does, why it's enough |
| 1:15–1:30 | Back to selfie, relaxed | the close + number |

**Filming:** two setups (selfie + one tripod position), about 15 minutes.
