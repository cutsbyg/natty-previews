# Lauvers Custom Construction

- **Business:** **Lauvers Custom Construction** — this is the name on Facebook and on their own
  site. The board calls them "Lauvers Exteriors"; **read the sign before putting a name in the
  header.** BuildZoom lists "Lauvers Exteriors" at the same address and the same phone, so both
  names are in the wild.
- **Trade:** total home remodeling / new construction (their own banner reads "Builds ·
  Additions · Remodeling · Roofing · Siding · Decks · All Construction Needs")
- **Town:** **Loysville, PA** — 1385 Rock Hollow Rd, 17047. Phone 717-275-6806. Not Newport.
- **FB URL:** https://www.facebook.com/lauverscustomconstruction/
- **Photo-set assessment:** **Deep but interior-heavy.** 66 photos came back — far more than the
  15 tiles the logged-out grid shows — but roughly forty of them are finished-interior real-estate
  style frames from one big whole-house remodel (grey walls, refinished floors, kitchens,
  bathrooms). Those make an excellent project grid and a poor hero. The exteriors are the good
  news: a heavy timber-frame porch going onto a ranch house (three stages of it), a green-sheathed
  garage under construction, a stone-walled gutted interior, and a green Victorian farmhouse.
  **Only one photo in all 66 shows a person working**, and his face is turned away.
