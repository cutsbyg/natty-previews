# Command Site Contractors, LLC

- **Business:** Command Site Contractors, LLC
- **Trade:** excavating / commercial site work (their own tailgate says "EXPERTS IN EXCAVATION")
- **Town:** Shermans Dale, PA — phone 717-582-0230
- **FB URL:** https://www.facebook.com/CommandSiteContractors
- **Photo-set assessment:** **The best photo set of the six, by a distance.** 63 real photos
  pulled logged-out, almost all genuine job-site work: hi-viz crews in open trenches, an
  operator on a roller, a surveyor shooting grade, their own COMMAND-lettered red dump truck
  being loaded by an excavator, stormwater chambers going in, a finished red-and-black
  basketball court. Resolution is **1440–2048px on nearly every frame** — no upscaling
  needed for a hero. Only 6 of the 63 are graphics (logo cards, a Thanksgiving card, two
  "We're Hiring" flyers) and one is a photo of kittens; those are all excluded from the sheet.
