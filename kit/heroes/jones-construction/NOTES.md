the best files are **2048x1494** (#5) and **960x960**; a lot of the 2021–22 album runs
**456x608 or 811x608**. #1 is 811x608 and #2 is 720x540 — both will need a faithful upscale
for a full-bleed hero. **Ask M. Jones for the originals at the door** (their email is
mjonesconstruction@hotmail.com).
