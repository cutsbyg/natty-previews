Questions we get asked.

What areas do you cover?

We are based in Elliottsburg in Perry County, and we take work across Cumberland, Dauphin, Franklin, Juniata, Mifflin and Perry counties, commercial as well as residential.

What kinds of flooring do you install?

Carpet, hardwood, luxury vinyl plank, laminate and resilient flooring, for homes and for commercial buildings.

Do you do bathrooms as well as floors?

Yes. We design and install custom tile showers and bathrooms, from the layout and the material through to the tile work itself.

Do you charge for an estimate?

No. We measure in your home for free and quote from those measurements.

Are you registered in Pennsylvania?

Yes. Our Pennsylvania Home Improvement Contractor registration number is PA048201.

Whose flooring can you get?

We are a Mohawk dealer, so we can get you a wide selection of hard and soft surface styles.
