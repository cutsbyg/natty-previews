Commonly asked questions

What does Command Site Contractors do?

Excavating and grading, site development, foundations, curb and flatwork, utility installation, light clearing and grubbing, and snow removal — residential and commercial.

Do you handle site development for a new build?

That is the core of the work: taking a raw lot through clearing, grading, utilities and foundation so it is ready to build on.

Do you do commercial and energy work?

Yes. Alongside residential and commercial site work, the energy side covers well pad construction, concrete foundations and supports, RUMA upgrades and maintenance, slip repairs, and erosion and maintenance crews.
