Commonly asked questions

Do you work with insurance?

Yes — all insurance companies. The shop handles the claims paperwork on its end, and every repair is warranted.

Do I have to use the shop my insurance tells me to use?

No. In Pennsylvania you have the absolute legal right to choose your own repair facility after an accident. Call us either way.

What do you do besides collision work?

Painting, frame and unibody repairs, glass replacement and paintless dent repair — foreign and domestic, every make and model.

Do you give free estimates?

Yes. Bring it by, or send photos through the form on this page and we will call you back.

Where are you, and what is nearby?

2410 South Market Street in Mechanicsburg, on PA 114. Mechanicsburg, Shiremanstown, Camp Hill, New Cumberland, Dillsburg, Boiling Springs, Carlisle and Harrisburg are all a short drive.

When are you open?

Weekdays, 8 in the morning to 5 in the afternoon. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
