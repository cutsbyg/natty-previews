Commonly asked questions

Do you do state inspection and emissions?

Yes — Dove’s is a licensed Pennsylvania inspection station, and emissions testing is done here too. Cars and medium trucks, safety and emissions both.

Do you specialise in any particular makes?

Honda, Acura and Subaru are the shop’s specialties. Every other make and model is welcome as well — domestic and import, cars and trucks.

Can you find out why my check engine light is on?

Bring it in. A trouble code names the circuit, not the part, so the work is finding out which one. The shop runs computer diagnostics and engine performance testing to get to the actual fault.

What else do you handle besides repairs?

Tune-ups, oil changes, tires and wheel alignments, brakes, exhaust, steering and suspension, air conditioning, filter changes, clutches and transmission work, and factory-recommended maintenance including timing belts.

How long have you been here?

Since 1979 — 47 years at it, and Better Business Bureau accredited since 2008.

Where are you, and when are you open?

101 Cumberland Parkway in Mechanicsburg, just off the Carlisle Pike. Monday through Friday, 7:30 in the morning until 5:00. Closed Saturday and Sunday.
